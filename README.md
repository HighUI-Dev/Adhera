# Adhera

A simple mobile and web app designed to help patients stay consistent with tuberculosis treatment and support doctors in monitoring adherence.

## 🧪 Demo Access

You can explore the app using built-in demo modes:

- **Demo Patient** → Track daily medication and view reminders  
- **Demo Doctor** → Monitor patient adherence and progress  

> Demo mode: changes are not saved.

---

## ✨ Features

- Track daily medication intake  
- Smart reminders for doses  
- Adherence tracking (streak + percentage)  
- Clear treatment phase explanations  
- Symptom logging  
- Basic patient–doctor follow-up  

---

## 📊 What the app focuses on

Tuberculosis treatment lasts several months.  
Patients often feel better early and may forget or stop their medication.

This app focuses on:
- making daily treatment simple  
- improving adherence  
- supporting better outcomes  

---

## 💡 Key Idea

> Consistency is the key to successful treatment
